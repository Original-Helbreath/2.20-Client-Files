Helbreath V2.02- THe Crusade.

v2.0 Contents
  
1. The Aresden,Elvine, and Middleland map
have been changed totally. And the map is
50% larger than before.

The size of the map,the interior and
exterior of the buildings, monster appearance location 
have been all changed. You will be able to see the 
difference between the before buildings and the actual.
The Beginner dungeon's location have been changed to
the city.
